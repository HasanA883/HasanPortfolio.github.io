Opti-Mind AI: AI-Powered Business Optimization Platform

OptiMind AI is an intelligent web application that helps businesses make optimal operational decisions without requiring knowledge of mathematical optimization or programming. Instead of manually creating linear programming models, users simply describe their business, products, resources, and objectives in plain language. The platform uses artificial intelligence to interpret the problem, identify the appropriate optimization model, formulate the mathematical equations, validate the model, solve it using a professional optimization engine, and present the results in an easy-to-understand format. The goal is to bridge the gap between advanced Operations Research techniques and everyday business decision-making.

Capital / Desk — Corporate Finance Workbench

Capital / Desk is a multi-module financial decision-support tool that walks through the full lifecycle of a capital investment decision: computing a firm's cost of capital (WACC), evaluating project NPV/IRR/payback with year-by-year discounted cash flow breakdowns, calculating CCA tax shields (including the half-year rule), comparing lease-vs-buy financing (NAL analysis), and generalizing the same discounting logic into a personal debt-payoff-vs-invest framework. Built to internalize concepts from an advanced corporate finance course by turning textbook formulas into a working analyst tool, complete with sensitivity analysis and a walkthrough mode that explains the reasoning behind each output rather than just displaying a number.
